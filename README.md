# Awesome Veteran Arsenal

**You earned it. Most of it is never handed to you — you have to claim it.**

Discharge paperwork goes in a drawer, and with it goes money, healthcare, tuition, housing help and survivor protection that was earned in service and is claimed by almost nobody in full. This list is the claim map: every major veteran benefit, right and resource, linked to the primary source that actually runs it — with the lever named: the form, the deadline, the exemption, the free help.

**Breachnode Field Guide № 3.** Maintained by [Breachnode](https://github.com/breachnode) — a hobbyist project sharing knowledge. Every claim on this page cites the agency or organization that administers it.

> ⚖️ **NOT LEGAL ADVICE, AND NOT THE VA.** This list is educational information about U.S. veterans benefits and rights, written for general use. It is **not** legal advice, it does not create an attorney–client relationship, and it is **not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, the Department of Defense, the SBA, or any agency linked here**. Benefits change — rates reset, programs open and close, legislatures amend. **Verify everything at the linked primary source (usually VA.gov) before you act**, and for a contested claim, a discharge upgrade, or serious money, use an accredited representative (see [Disability Compensation & Appeals](#-disability-compensation--appeals)).

> 🛑 **The two never-pay rules:** **Never pay for a DD214** — copies are free from the National Archives for veterans and next of kin. **Never pay anyone to file an initial VA claim** — accredited VSO help is always free, and accredited attorneys/agents legally cannot charge for an initial claim. Anyone breaking either rule is the subject of [Fraud Watch](#-fraud-watch), not a shortcut.

> **The border of this list:** what you *earned* and how to claim it lives here. Prevention, verification and the first 72 hours after a scam live in **[Awesome Scam Defense](https://github.com/breachnode/awesome-scam-defense)** (Breachnode Field Guide № 1). The long institutional consumer fight — credit disputes, collectors, chargebacks, regulators — lives in **[Awesome Consumer Revenge Kit](https://github.com/breachnode/awesome-consumer-revenge-kit)** (Breachnode Field Guide № 2).

> 🧟 **Zombie programs — dead, replaced, or changed. Do not let an old guide send you here:**
> - **The original VET TEC pilot (2019–2024) is over.** Its certificates and provider approvals are void. The live program is [VET TEC 2.0](#-education) — and it is *different*: it charges GI Bill entitlement, is capped by fiscal year, and is funded through September 30, 2027.
> - **eBenefits is retired.** Everything moved to VA.gov. Any instruction that starts "log in to eBenefits" is stale.
> - **DS Logon and My HealtheVet passwords no longer sign you in to VA.gov.** Current options are CLEAR, ID.me, and Login.gov. (My HealtheVet *services* survive inside VA.gov; the old sign-in does not.)
> - **"New and material evidence" is dead language.** For decisions on or after February 19, 2019, the Appeals Modernization Act lanes govern, and a Supplemental Claim needs **new and *relevant*** evidence.
> - **The Veterans Choice Program ended June 6, 2019.** The MISSION Act's Veterans Community Care Program replaced it — the old "40-mile rule" is not the standard anymore.
> - **REAP (Chapter 1607) ended in November 2015**, and the Post-9/11 GI Bill's old 40% tier is gone — the floor is 50% at 90 days of service. Both still appear in benefit roundups; neither can be claimed.
> - **VA does not certify veteran businesses anymore.** Certification moved from VA's CVE to the SBA's VetCert on January 1, 2023, and self-certification for VA set-asides ended with it.
> - **The SBP–DIC offset ("widow's tax") was fully eliminated in January 2023.** Any source still subtracting DIC from SBP is pre-2023 and wrong.
> - **VA's old state-offices directory page is dead** — it now redirects to a generic About page. The living directory is [NASDVA](#-state-benefits).

## How to use this list

- **No DD214, no benefits.** Start with [Start Here](#-start-here--verify-status--records): records, a VA.gov sign-in, and your profile — everything else keys off these.
- **Filing or fighting a rating?** [Disability Compensation & Appeals](#-disability-compensation--appeals) — file the intent to file, take the free accredited help, and learn the fee rules before anyone quotes you a price.
- **Sick, or care is far away?** [Healthcare](#-healthcare) — enrollment first, then community care, urgent care, and the 72-hour emergency rule.
- **School or a trade?** [Education](#-education) — compare schools with VA's own tool, and check the *current* rate table; rates reset every August 1.
- **Buying a home?** [Home Loans & Housing](#-home-loans--housing) — get the COE, and check the funding-fee exemption list *before* closing, not after.
- **Buying something anyway?** [Shopping & Savings](#-shopping--savings) — the discount programs with their caps and exclusions stated, starting with the tax-free one.
- **Free time to fill?** [Lesser-Known Benefits](#-lesser-known-benefits) — the quiet perks: passes, tickets, base privileges, and the directories that find your state and county layer.
- **Someone is charging you for help?** [Fraud Watch](#-fraud-watch). Verify their accreditation, then report them.

**Legend:** `Free` · `Freemium` = free tier is genuinely usable · `Paid` · 📋 = a *play* — an action, a deadline or a status check, not a tool. (Almost everything here is free by design; a cost tag appears only where a real cost exists.)

**Inclusion bar:** every entry links to a primary source — VA.gov, the administering agency, the statute, or the organization's own site — and states the lever it gives you. No affiliate links. No sponsored placements. No lead-generation "benefits" sites. Full criteria in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

**Rates rule for this list:** dollar figures that reset on a schedule — disability compensation (every December 1), GI Bill rates (every August 1), grant maximums (every fiscal year) — are **linked, not quoted**, unless a figure is labeled with its period. A precise number with no date is how old guides lie.

## 🗂️ Start Here — Verify Status & Records

Every benefit in this list keys off two things: proof of service, and a working VA.gov sign-in. Get both before you need either.

- **[Get military service records (including DD214) — VA](https://www.va.gov/records/get-military-service-records/)** — VA's front door to your DD214 and service records; it routes you to the right custodian, and if you're filing for benefits, VA can request the DD214 itself. `Free`
- **[OMPF access / eVetRecs — National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/personnel-records-center/ompf-access)** — The actual records custodian (NPRC, St. Louis): request online through eVetRecs or by SF-180; basic records are generally free to veterans and next of kin. `Free`
- **[Signing in to VA.gov](https://www.va.gov/resources/signing-in-to-vagov/)** — One sign-in runs claims, healthcare, education and the COE: VA.gov now accepts CLEAR, ID.me, or Login.gov accounts — DS Logon and My HealtheVet passwords no longer work here. `Free`
- **[Manage your VA.gov profile](https://www.va.gov/resources/managing-your-vagov-profile/)** — Fix contact information and see your linked service and personal data in one place; stale contact info is how decision letters and exam notices go missing. `Free`
- **[milConnect — DoD/DMDC](https://milconnect.dmdc.osd.mil/milconnect/)** — DEERS data, benefit-transfer requests, and records for recent separations; note that milConnect sign-in is moving to myAuth, replacing DS Logon — follow the site's current prompt, not an old walkthrough. `Free`

## 🏅 Disability Compensation & Appeals

The claim is the benefit. File it right — intent to file, real evidence, free accredited help — and a decision you disagree with is a fork with three marked lanes, not a wall.

- **[How to file a VA disability claim](https://www.va.gov/disability/how-to-file-claim/)** — File online (Form 21-526EZ) and your intent to file is captured automatically; paper filers should submit an intent to file first — it protects your effective date while you gather evidence for up to a year. `Free`
- **[Evidence needed for your disability claim](https://www.benefits.va.gov/COMPENSATION/evidence.asp)** — Exactly what VA wants: DD214, service treatment records, current medical evidence, and buddy/lay statements — evidence matched to this checklist is what gets claims decided instead of deferred. `Free`
- **[Current disability compensation rates](https://www.va.gov/disability/compensation-rates/veteran-rates/)** — The only rate table to trust: VA's own, currently the 2026 table effective December 1, 2025. Rates reset every **December 1** with the Social Security COLA — never copy a dollar figure out of a guide, including this one. `Free`
- **[VA claim exams (C&P exams)](https://www.benefits.va.gov/compensation/claimexam.asp)** — What a compensation & pension exam is, who runs it (VA's contractor examiners), and how to prepare — the exam report often decides the rating, so treat the appointment like the claim depends on it. It may. `Free`
- **[Choosing a decision review option](https://www.va.gov/resources/choosing-a-decision-review-option/)** — VA's router for the three Appeals Modernization lanes, with its own time estimates for each — read this before picking a lane, because the lanes take different evidence and different clocks. `Free`
- **[Higher-Level Review](https://www.va.gov/decision-reviews/higher-level-review/)** — A senior reviewer re-checks the *same* record for error; no new evidence allowed. Request within **1 year** of the decision (Form 20-0996); one informal conference is optional. `Free`
- **[Supplemental Claim](https://www.va.gov/decision-reviews/supplemental-claim/)** — The lane for **new and relevant** evidence — VA will help gather it. File within **1 year** of the decision to protect the effective date (Form 20-0995); it's also the lane for newly presumptive conditions. `Free`
- **[Board Appeal](https://www.va.gov/decision-reviews/board-appeal)** — A Veterans Law Judge reviews your case on one of three dockets — Direct Review, Evidence Submission, or Hearing. Request within **1 year** (Form 10182); after a Board decision, the next doors are a Supplemental Claim or the Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims within 120 days. `Free`
- **[Get help from an accredited representative or VSO](https://www.va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative)** — VA's own page, in VA's words: VSO representative services are **always free**; an accredited attorney or claims agent *can* charge — but only after VA's initial decision, under the fee rules. Appoint with Form 21-22 (VSO) or 21-22a (attorney/agent). `Free`
- **[OGC Accreditation Search](https://www.va.gov/ogc/apps/accreditation/index.asp)** — Verify any representative is actually VA-accredited before signing anything: VA's warning is blunt — no result in this search means not currently authorized to represent you. `Free`
- **[VA Claims & Separation Benefits — VFW](https://www.vfw.org/assistance/va-claims-separation-benefits)** — Free claims help from VFW accredited service officers, including pre-discharge help near separation — no charge, ever, for the service. `Free`
- **[VA Benefits Help — DAV](https://www.dav.org/get-help-now/va-benefits-help/)** — DAV benefits experts counsel veterans on claims and appeals at no cost and stay with the claim through the process — no membership required for service. `Free`
- **[Find a Service Officer — The American Legion](https://www.legion.org/serviceofficers)** — The locator for the Legion's accredited service officers, including dedicated appeals teams; the help is free whether or not you're a member. `Free`
- **[National Service Offices — Paralyzed Veterans of America](https://pva.org/find-support/national-service-office/)** — Free service-officer help for **all** veterans, with unmatched depth on spinal cord injury/disease, MS and ALS claims — the specialist call when a claim turns on those conditions. `Free`

## ❤️ Healthcare

Enrollment is the gate; everything else — community care, urgent care, emergency coverage, travel pay — hangs off being enrolled and knowing which rule applies before you go.

- **[How to apply for VA health care](https://www.va.gov/health-care/how-to-apply/)** — Enroll with Form 10-10EZ online, by phone, by mail, in person, or through an accredited representative — five doors, one enrollment. `Free`
- **[Eligibility for VA health care](https://www.va.gov/health-care/eligibility/)** — The basic test: qualifying service and a discharge other than dishonorable — and since March 2024, toxic-exposed veterans can enroll directly under the PACT Act, so eligibility is broader than older guides say. `Free`
- **[VA priority groups](https://www.va.gov/health-care/eligibility/priority-groups/)** — Your group (1–8) sets enrollment order and copays — Group 1 starts at 50%+ service-connected; check where you land before assuming a bill is correct. `Free`
- **[VA community care network and covered services](https://www.va.gov/resources/about-our-va-community-care-network-and-covered-services/)** — The MISSION Act program that pays for outside care when VA can't provide it timely or nearby: you generally need a referral and authorization first — get the authorization *before* the appointment, not after the bill. `Free`
- **[Urgent care at VA or in-network community providers](https://www.va.gov/resources/getting-urgent-care-at-va-or-in-network-community-providers)** — The walk-in urgent care benefit: minor illness or injury, **in-network only, no prior approval needed** — out-of-network urgent care is the expensive misunderstanding this page prevents. `Free`
- **[Emergency care at non-VA facilities](https://www.va.gov/resources/getting-emergency-care-at-non-va-facilities/)** — A real emergency needs no prior approval — go — but **VA must be notified within 72 hours** of care starting, or coverage gets hard. Emergency mental-health care is covered even if you're not enrolled. `Free`
- **[File a travel reimbursement claim (BTSSS)](https://www.va.gov/health-care/file-travel-pay-reimbursement/)** — Get paid back for travel to VA care — file in the Beneficiary Travel Self Service System **within 30 days** of the appointment, and note travel pay needs its own direct-deposit setup. `Free`

## 🎓 Education

The GI Bill is a budget with an expiration date, not a coupon. Compare the school, check the current rates, and — if you might transfer benefits — understand that the transfer door closes at separation.

- **[Post-9/11 GI Bill (Chapter 33)](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/post-9-11/)** — The main benefit, explained by VA. Note: the "Forever GI Bill" is not a separate program you apply for — it's the 2017 law whose changes live inside these pages, chiefly no expiration if your last separation was on or after January 1, 2013. `Free`
- **[Post-9/11 GI Bill rates](https://www.va.gov/education/benefit-rates/post-9-11-gi-bill-rates/)** — The official tuition and housing rate tables — the current period runs **August 1, 2026 – July 31, 2027**, and rates reset every August 1. Link it, check it, never trust a screenshot of last year's numbers. `Free`
- **[GI Bill Comparison Tool](https://www.va.gov/education/gi-bill-comparison-tool/)** — Compare schools on real tuition and housing estimates, graduate outcomes and VA caution flags *before* enrolling — the cheapest quality check in education is this page, used early. `Free`
- **[Transfer your Post-9/11 GI Bill benefits](https://www.va.gov/education/transfer-post-9-11-gi-bill-benefits/)** — Unused months can go to a spouse or children, but the request runs through milConnect **while you are still serving**, with a service commitment attached — waiting until after separation is permanently too late. `Free`
- **[VR&E — how to apply (Chapter 31)](https://www.va.gov/careers-employment/vocational-rehabilitation/how-to-apply/)** — Training plus employment services for veterans whose service-connected disability limits work: apply with Form 28-1900, and a Vocational Rehabilitation Counselor decides entitlement — a different, often stronger benefit than the GI Bill for the same classroom. `Free`
- **[VET TEC 2.0](https://www.va.gov/education/other-va-education-benefits/vet-tec-2/)** — Live and accepting applications: non-degree tech training (programming, data, information sciences), funded through **September 30, 2027** with a per-fiscal-year participant cap. Read VA's fine print first: unlike the dead pilot, it **charges GI Bill entitlement** — check the page for current openings. `Free`
- **[Edith Nourse Rogers STEM Scholarship](https://www.va.gov/education/other-va-education-benefits/stem-scholarship/)** — Up to 9 extra months of benefits when you're running out of Post-9/11 entitlement in a qualifying STEM program — narrow gates (including how little benefit you must have left), so apply before the balance hits zero, not after. `Free`
- **[VA Work-Study](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/how-to-use-benefits/work-study/)** — Paid work at VA-related sites while you're in school at least three-quarter time — an allowance most students never claim because nobody mentions it during enrollment. `Free`

## 💼 Employment & Transition

Transition is a paperwork campaign: rights that survive discharge (USERRA), programs with windows that close (SkillBridge, transfer of entitlement), and a federal hiring system that rewards those who claim preference correctly.

- **[Transition Assistance Program (TAP)](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/vets/programs/tap)** — The interagency transition curriculum — DOL's employment portion is the part with deadlines and deliverables; spouses are covered too, and starting early is the whole game. `Free`
- **[DoD SkillBridge](https://skillbridge.osd.mil/)** — Civilian training and internships with approved partners during your final stretch of service — the official homepage's window is 180 days or fewer remaining, but branch policy and command approval decide your real eligibility, so check both. `Free`
- **[USERRA — DOL VETS](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/vets/programs/userra)** — Reemployment rights for Guard and Reserve service: what your civilian employer owes you, and where to get claim help when they don't deliver. `Free`
- **[USERRA statute (38 U.S.C. Chapter 43)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/chapter-43)** — The law itself, §§ 4301–4335 — when an employer's HR department offers its own summary of your rights, this is the document that outranks the summary. `Free`
- **[ESGR — Employer Support of the Guard and Reserve](https://www.esgr.mil/)** — DoD's free, informal route: USERRA education and ombudsman mediation with your employer *before* a formal DOL claim — many disputes end here because nobody had explained the rule. `Free`
- **[OPM Vet Guide for HR Professionals](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/veterans-services/vet-guide-for-hr-professionals/)** — The authoritative rules for 5- and 10-point preference and special appointing authorities — written for HR, which makes it the exact standard to hold an HR decision against. `Free`
- **[USAJOBS — veterans hiring path](https://www.usajobs.gov/help/working-in-government/unique-hiring-paths/veterans/)** — How preference points and veterans-only announcements actually work in the federal job system — claim the preference on every application; it is never applied retroactively. `Free`
- **[VA Careers and Employment](https://www.va.gov/careers-employment/)** — VA's employment hub: VR&E tracks, job help, and Personalized Career Planning and Guidance — free career and education counseling that doesn't require a disability rating. `Free`
- **[Registered Apprenticeship for veterans](https://www.apprenticeship.gov/help/what-are-benefits-apprenticeship-veterans-and-service-members)** — Paid apprenticeship wages, with GI Bill housing allowance potentially on top — the catch is in the fine print: the specific program must be VA-approved, so verify approval before you sign. `Free`
- **[CareerOneStop Veteran and Military Transition Center](https://careeronestop.org/Veterans/)** — DOL-sponsored translator between military and civilian careers: job matching by military occupation, training finder, and local-help locator. `Free`
- **[Veterans Priority of Service — American Job Centers](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/jobs-for-veterans-act)** — In every DOL-funded job and training program, veterans and eligible spouses go first — 2,400+ American Job Centers run on this rule; if a local program doesn't know it, this page is the citation. `Free`
- **[License portability (50 U.S.C. § 4025a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/50/4025a)** — Relocating on orders? A covered professional license in good standing must be treated as valid in the new state when you apply with your orders — the federal lever behind the state-by-state patchwork, for servicemembers and spouses. `Free`

## 🏠 Home Loans & Housing

The VA loan is usually the single most valuable financial benefit on this page — and its two most-missed details are both free to check: whether you owe the funding fee at all, and which loan-limit rule actually applies to you.

- **[VA home loans](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/)** — The hub for VA-backed and VA direct purchase, refinance and improvement loans, and how entitlement works — start here, then get the COE. `Free`
- **[Home loan eligibility](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/eligibility/)** — Minimum service requirements by era, Guard/Reserve rules, surviving-spouse eligibility, and restoring entitlement after a prior loan — the gate every lender checks first. `Free`
- **[Request a Certificate of Eligibility](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/request-coe-form-26-1880/)** — Apply for the COE online (Form 26-1880), or through your lender or by mail — get it early: the COE's entitlement figures decide your down-payment math. `Free`
- **[Funding fee and closing costs](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/)** — Check the exemption list **before closing**: no fee if you're receiving VA disability compensation, eligible for it but drawing retirement/active pay instead, a surviving spouse receiving DIC, or in the pre-discharge/Purple Heart categories VA lists — and there's a refund route if compensation lands retroactively. `Free`
- **[Entitlement and loan limits](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-limits/)** — VA's current rule, in its own terms: with **full entitlement you have no VA loan limit** (the lender's approval and the appraisal still govern); county limits return only when you have *remaining* entitlement — ignore any guide that quotes county caps as universal law. `Free`
- **[Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan (IRRRL)](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/interest-rate-reduction-loan/)** — The streamline refinance of an **existing VA-backed loan only** — and VA itself warns on this page about misleading "skip payments" refinance offers; compare any pitch against it. `Free`
- **[Native American Direct Loan (NADL)](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/native-american-direct-loan/)** — VA lends directly for buying, building or improving on federal trust land, for Native American veterans (or veterans married to a Native American): generally no down payment, no PMI, limited closing costs — and the rate on the page is current-only, so read it there. `Free`
- **[Disability housing grants (SAH / SHA / TRA)](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/disability-housing-grants/)** — Non-repayable grants to buy, build or adapt a home around qualifying service-connected disabilities, including adapting a family member's home you live in temporarily — maximums are set per fiscal year (the page shows the current FY figures), and narrow qualifying-disability lists apply. `Free`
- **[VA homeless programs](https://www.va.gov/homeless/)** — One front door to HUD-VASH vouchers with case management, SSVF homelessness prevention and rapid re-housing, and Grant and Per Diem transitional housing — three different programs behind one hub; start here or with the call center below. `Free`
- **[Homeless help — National Call Center for Homeless Veterans](https://www.va.gov/resources/homeless-help/)** — **1-877-4AID-VET (1-877-424-3838)**: free, confidential, 24/7 — connects homeless and at-risk veterans to their nearest VA help; the call center routes you, it doesn't itself hand out beds or vouchers. `Free`

## 💵 Money & Debt

Service comes with its own financial rulebook — interest-rate caps, reemployment-linked protections, and a VA debt system with forgiveness built in. Most of it only works if you invoke it, in writing, inside its window.

- **[CFPB — Servicemembers, Veterans & Military Families](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/servicemembers/)** — The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's military hub: SCRA and Military Lending Act explainers, common scams, and the complaint route that companies answer. `Free`
- **[CFPB — Military Financial Lifecycle](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/military-financial-lifecycle/)** — Stage-by-stage money guides — enlistment, deployment, transition, veteran issues — including the pension-advance traps aimed at retirees. `Free`
- **[SCRA Website — DMDC](https://scra.dmdc.osd.mil/)** — The Defense Department's official active-duty status verification: the certificate lenders, landlords and courts use to confirm Servicemembers Civil Relief Act protections apply. (A verification system, not a rights guide — pair it with the CFPB hub above.) `Free`
- **[DOJ Servicemembers and Veterans Initiative](https://www.justice.gov/servicemembers)** — Where SCRA, USERRA and military voting/financial-rights violations get reported to the agency that enforces them — the escalation when a lender or landlord ignores the statute. `Free`
- **[Military Lending Act Website — DMDC](https://mla.dmdc.osd.mil/)** — The official covered-borrower verification behind the MLA's 36% military APR cap regime — check a borrower's status here; the plain-English explainer lives on the CFPB hub. `Free`
- **[Options to request help with VA debt](https://www.va.gov/resources/options-to-request-help-with-va-debt/)** — Overpayment or copay debt isn't take-it-or-leave-it: repayment plan, **waiver (forgiveness)**, compromise offer, hardship suspension, or dispute. The clocks are real — dispute within **30 days** to pause collection, and a waiver request is due within **1 year** of the first debt letter. `Free`
- **[Armed Forces Legal Assistance locator](https://legalassistance.law.af.mil/)** — Find the nearest military legal assistance office for free help with SCRA questions, wills and consumer problems — the joint-service locator, hosted by the Air Force JAG. `Free`
- **[Beneficiary Financial Counseling Service — VA Insurance](https://www.benefits.va.gov/INSURANCE/bfcs.asp)** — Free professional financial counseling and online will preparation — with the narrow gate stated plainly: only for SGLI/TSGLI/FSGLI/VGLI beneficiaries, for two years after a claim payment. It is not a general counseling benefit; don't let anyone sell it as one. `Free`

## 🛒 Shopping & Savings

These are commercial programs, not VA benefits — so each entry states its real terms: who qualifies, the cap, the exclusions, the enrollment step. A "military discount" headline with none of those defined didn't make the cut. And a discount is a starting price, not the lowest one: compare the final number — starting with the tax-free option at the top — before you check out.

- **[Veterans Online Shopping Benefit — The Exchange](https://www.shopmyexchange.com/vets)** — Every honorably discharged veteran can shop the Army & Air Force Exchange online **for life**: tax-free, with military-exclusive pricing, after a one-time service verification. Online only — it does not grant base access (in-person privileges are the separate 2020 expansion in [Lesser-Known Benefits](#-lesser-known-benefits)). The Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard exchanges run the same online benefit.
- **[ID.me Shop](https://shop.id.me)** — The verification layer under half this section: verify your military status once with ID.me, then unlock member pricing at hundreds of brands without re-proving service at every checkout. Shop is ID.me's own deals directory — each brand sets its own discount and terms, and ID.me discloses that it earns affiliate commissions there. `Free`
- **[GovX](https://www.govx.com)** — A members-only marketplace for current and former military, first responders and government employees: free membership after GovX ID verification, member pricing on gear, apparel, travel and tickets. Prices vary by brand and are not automatically the lowest — compare against the tax-free Exchange above before buying. `Free`
- **[The Home Depot — military discount](https://www.homedepot.com/c/military)** — 10% off eligible purchases for service members, veterans and spouses, online and in store — with the cap stated up front: a **$400 maximum discount per calendar year**, resetting each January. Enroll once through your Home Depot account or app; exclusions include appliances, commodity building materials, special orders, tool rental, labor, delivery and gift cards.
- **[Lowe's — military discount](https://www.lowes.com/military)** — 10% off most full-priced items in store and online for verified service members, veterans and spouses — and unlike its competitor, Lowe's terms set **no annual cap**. Requires a MyLowe's Rewards account with ID.me verification (spouses enroll separately); excluded: sale items, major appliances and parts, lumber and other commodities, gift cards and services.
- **[Verizon — military discount](https://www.verizon.com/military/)** — Monthly savings on eligible mobile plans, Fios internet and accessories for active duty, retirees, veterans, current or former Guard and Reserve, and Gold Star families. The mobile amount is plan-dependent — flat account credits on current unlimited plans, not one flat percentage — registered to your account once through ID.me; Verizon may occasionally ask you to re-verify.
- **[T-Mobile — military plans](https://www.t-mobile.com/cell-phone-plans/military-discount-plans)** — Dedicated military-priced versions of its plans for service members, veterans, Guard/Reserve and Gold Star families, with the deepest savings on multi-line accounts. Two conditions that void the deal if missed: the military member must be the **primary account holder**, and status must be verified **within 45 days** of activation.
- **[AT&T — military discount](https://www.att.com/offers/discount-program/military-discount/)** — 10–20% off AT&T's current unlimited wireless plans, the percentage set by plan tier, on up to 10 lines — the military member must be the primary account holder and pass AT&T's verification (documents within 14 days if asked). Older write-ups quote 25%; that figure belonged to retired plan names.
- **[Armed Forces Vacation Club](https://www.afvclub.com/new-member-welcome)** — Free membership for current, former and retired military, Guard/Reserve, Gold Star families, DoD civilians and their immediate families: discounted resort stays, hotels and cruises, headlined by flat-rate week-long resort certificates. It is a commercial travel club (run on the RCI network), not a VA benefit — compare the total with taxes and resort fees before booking. `Free`
- **[PatriotStoreDirect — Veterans Canteen Service (VA)](https://www.vacanteen.va.gov/VACANTEEN/specialorder.asp)** — VA's own retailer, inside VA medical centers since 1946: tax-free, discounted name-brand goods (tires are the long-running specialty) sold online exclusively to veterans, their families, caregivers and VA employees — ordering runs through web forms and customer service, not a modern storefront. Its tickets-and-travel deals are for veterans **enrolled in VA health care**.

## 📈 Small Business

Veteran-owned is a certification, not a self-description — at least in federal contracting, where the SBA now runs the gate. Training and local counseling, on the other hand, are free and underused.

- **[SBA veteran business resources](https://www.sba.gov/veterans)** — The Small Business Administration's single hub for veteran entrepreneurs — funding, training, outreach centers and contracting — run by its Office of Veterans Business Development. SBA also anchors the **Vets First** rule here: set-aside and sole-source VA contracts require SBA certification (next entry). `Free`
- **[Veteran Small Business Certification (VetCert)](https://certifications.sba.gov/)** — Apply for — or verify — the VOSB/SDVOSB certification now required for federal set-aside and sole-source contracts. Certification transferred from VA to the SBA on **January 1, 2023**; self-certification no longer counts for VA work. `Free`
- **[Boots to Business — SBA](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/boots-to-business/)** — The free two-day introduction-to-entrepreneurship course: on installations as a TAP track for transitioning members and spouses, and as **Reboot** off-installation for veterans of all eras. `Free`
- **[Veterans Business Outreach Centers (VBOCs) — SBA](https://www.sba.gov/local-assistance/resource-partners/veterans-business-outreach-center-vboc-program)** — The directory of local centers offering free counseling, business-plan help and mentorship — the SBA's help, delivered in your state instead of on a website. `Free`
- **[VetFran](https://www.vetfran.org)** — The franchise industry's veteran program: a directory of brands offering franchise-fee discounts and incentives, plus franchising education. Know what it is — an industry (non-government) program, discounts vary by brand, and a franchise is an investment, not a grant. `Free`

## 🪦 Retirement, Burial & Survivors

The benefits families scramble for at the worst moment are mostly arrangeable in advance — eligibility decisions, markers, honors — and the pay questions (retired pay vs. VA compensation) have exact rules that old summaries routinely get wrong.

- **[DFAS — who qualifies for military retirement](https://www.dfas.mil/RetiredMilitary/plan/eligibility/)** — The pay agency's own gate: 20+ years, medical retirement, or Reserve retirement at 60 with 20 qualifying years — DFAS, not VA, pays retired pay, so start pay questions here. `Free`
- **[Concurrent Retirement and Disability Pay (CRDP) — DFAS](https://www.dfas.mil/retiredmilitary/disability/crdp/)** — Restores retired pay that was waived for VA compensation, for retirees rated **50% or more** — in most cases DFAS applies it automatically from VA data; no application. `Free`
- **[Combat-Related Special Compensation (CRSC) — DFAS](https://www.dfas.mil/retiredmilitary/disability/crsc/)** — Tax-free concurrent pay for combat-related disabilities rated 10%+, including many disability retirees CRDP excludes — but it is **not** automatic: you apply to your branch of service, and you can't draw CRDP and CRSC at once. `Free`
- **[Survivor Benefit Plan (SBP) — DFAS](https://www.dfas.mil/RetiredMilitary/provide/sbp/)** — The DoD annuity that continues a share of retired pay to a survivor, elected at retirement — and hard to change afterward. Note for survivors: the old SBP–DIC offset is gone (eliminated January 2023); SBP is taxable to the survivor, DIC is not. `Free`
- **[Veterans burial allowance](https://www.va.gov/burials-memorials/veterans-burial-allowance/)** — Reimbursement toward burial, funeral, plot and transportation costs (Form 21P-530EZ) — a partial reimbursement, not a direct payment, and non-service-connected claims carry a **2-year filing deadline**; amounts adjust, so read the current figures on the page. `Free`
- **[Eligibility for burial in a VA national cemetery](https://www.va.gov/burials-memorials/eligibility/)** — The National Cemetery Administration's rule-set for veterans, spouses and dependents — the page to check *before* a family assumes, or is told, there's no eligibility. `Free`
- **[Schedule a burial](https://www.va.gov/burials-memorials/schedule-a-burial/)** — The at-need route runs through the National Cemetery Scheduling Office, not the cemetery itself — and a specific gravesite or cemetery can't be reserved in advance, which is exactly why the pre-need entry below exists. `Free`
- **[Headstones, markers and medallions](https://www.va.gov/burials-memorials/memorial-items/headstones-markers-medallions/)** — A free government headstone or marker for an unmarked grave anywhere in the world (Form 40-1330), or a medallion *instead of* one for a private headstone — send copies of discharge papers, never originals. `Free`
- **[Presidential Memorial Certificates](https://www.va.gov/burials-memorials/memorial-items/presidential-memorial-certificates/)** — Free engraved certificates signed by the sitting President (Form 40-0247); they arrive automatically for national-cemetery burials — families using a private cemetery must apply, and multiple copies are allowed. `Free`
- **[Burial flags](https://www.va.gov/burials-memorials/memorial-items/burial-flags/)** — One free U.S. flag per veteran to drape the casket or accompany the urn (Form 27-2008), via a funeral director, VA regional office or Post Office — by law there is only one, and VA cannot replace a lost one. `Free`
- **[Military funeral honors](https://www.cem.va.gov/cem/military_funeral_honors.asp)** — Honors on request: at minimum two uniformed members, Taps, flag folding and presentation — arrange through the funeral director 48–72 hours ahead, and never pay a family fee for what DoD provides. `Free`
- **[Pre-need eligibility for burial in a VA cemetery](https://www.va.gov/burials-memorials/pre-need-eligibility/)** — Get the eligibility decision **while alive** (Form 40-10007) so nobody is hunting for a DD214 at the worst moment — the decision doesn't guarantee a specific cemetery or gravesite, and VA re-validates at time of death. `Free`
- **[Dependency and Indemnity Compensation (DIC)](https://www.va.gov/family-and-caregiver-benefits/survivor-compensation/dependency-indemnity-compensation/)** — Tax-free monthly payments for surviving spouses, children and dependent parents after a service-connected or line-of-duty death (with the long-held-total-rating routes VA lists) — distinct from Survivors Pension and from SBP; VA considers DIC and Pension together and pays one. `Free`
- **[Survivors Pension](https://www.va.gov/family-and-caregiver-benefits/survivor-compensation/survivors-pension/)** — Needs-based monthly payments for qualifying surviving spouses and dependent children of **wartime** veterans, under congressional income and net-worth limits — unlike DIC, this one is means-tested, and the means test is what pension poachers exploit (see [Fraud Watch](#-fraud-watch)). `Free`
- **[Aid and Attendance and Housebound](https://www.va.gov/pension/aid-attendance-housebound/)** — A higher pension rate — not a standalone program — when a pensioner needs help with daily activities, is bedridden or in a nursing home, or is substantially home-confined; you can't draw A&A and Housebound at the same time. `Free`

## 🌎 State Benefits

Every state runs a second, separate benefits system on top of the federal one — property-tax exemptions, tuition programs, licenses, state cemeteries, bonus payments — and no honest list can print 50 states' rules in one page. These are the official doors.

- **[NASDVA — State & Territory Directory](https://nasdva.us/resources/)** — The National Association of State Directors of Veterans Affairs' directory: every state and territory veterans agency, with its current director — the one link that routes you to your own state's tax, tuition, home and claims help. `Free`
- **[NASDVA](https://www.nasdva.us/)** — The state directors' own association (est. 1946) — the body behind the directory above, confirming it is the states' official channel and not a commercial aggregator. State benefits vary entirely by state of residence; claim the federal floor first, then this second layer. `Free`

## 🎁 Lesser-Known Benefits

The headline benefits get the brochures; these get discovered in bar conversations a decade late — free passes, free tickets, base privileges created by a 2020 law change, and the local layer. That layer is real but hyper-local, so it appears here as directories and checks, not listings: any site printing "your city's veteran perks" from a national template is inventing them.

- **[Military passes — America the Beautiful (NPS)](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/passes.htm)** — Two different free passes, often confused: veterans and Gold Star Families get a free **lifetime** Military Pass; current service members and dependents get a free **annual** one. Both waive entrance and standard day-use fees at 2,000+ federal recreation sites. Bring ID or a DD214 to a staffed site; camping, tours and reservation fees are not covered. `Free`
- **[Access Pass — America the Beautiful (NPS)](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/passes.htm)** — The other lifetime pass: free for U.S. citizens and permanent residents with a medically documented permanent disability — a VA disability rating counts. Same entrance coverage as the military passes, and it is the one that may also cut some expanded amenity fees (camping, boat launch) by 50%. `Free`
- **[Commissary, exchange and MWR privileges (VA)](https://www.va.gov/resources/commissary-and-exchange-privileges-for-veterans/)** — The 2020 expansion most veterans never heard about: **any** service-connected disability rating — not just 100% — plus Purple Heart recipients, former POWs and designated caregivers can shop commissaries, exchanges and MWR retail in person. No application; show a VHIC marked SERVICE CONNECTED, or a VA letter plus photo ID. Online exchange shopping: [Shopping & Savings](#-shopping--savings). `Free`
- **[Vet Tix — Veteran Tickets Foundation](https://www.vettix.org/visitor/the-team/about)** — Free tickets to sports, concerts, theater and family events, donated by teams, venues and promoters. A national 501(c)(3): currently serving members, honorably discharged veterans of all eras, and immediate family of those killed in action register after service verification. Tickets are free; a small delivery fee applies. `Free`
- **[Blue Star Museums (NEA)](https://www.arts.gov/initiatives/blue-star-museums/frequently-asked-questions)** — Free museum admission each summer (Armed Forces Day–Labor Day) at 2,000+ museums — but read the scope: it covers **currently serving** members, including Guard and Reserve, plus up to five family members, with a CAC or dependent ID. Veterans and retirees are *not* included; the NEA's own advice is to ask the museum directly — many run their own veteran rates. `Free`
- **[County Veteran Service Officers — NACVSO](https://www.nacvso.org)** — Your county employs an accredited service officer whose whole job is your benefits: claims help, state and local programs, and the perks no national list can publish. Free, local, and VA-recognized for claims representation; NACVSO's "Find a Service Officer" directory locates yours. This is also the fastest route to city and county perks where you actually live. `Free`
- **[Stateside Legal](https://www.statesidelegal.org)** — Free civil legal help beyond the base legal office: a national directory of free and low-cost legal aid for veterans, service members and families — benefits, housing, family law, discharge issues — searchable by state or ZIP, run by Pine Tree Legal Assistance. For on-base help, see the legal assistance locator in [Money & Debt](#-money--debt). `Free`
- **Your state and county layer** 📋 — Many states waive state-park day-use fees or discount annual passes for veterans (terms vary wildly; some are free for disabled veterans), and public libraries commonly lend free museum and park passes. Neither perk can be listed nationally without inventing it: route through your [state DVA](#-state-benefits) for the park pass, and your library's pass program for the rest.

## 🆘 Help & Crisis

Crisis help is not gated behind enrollment, eligibility reviews or business hours. If this section is the reason you opened the list, start at the first entry.

- **[Veterans Crisis Line](https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/)** — **Dial 988, then Press 1. Text 838255. Or chat online.** Free, confidential, 24/7 — no VA enrollment required, for veterans, service members, Guard/Reserve, and the people who support them. Old guides headline the pre-2022 number; 988 is the front door now. `Free`
- **[VA mental health services](https://www.va.gov/health-care/health-needs-conditions/mental-health/)** — VA's hub for PTSD, depression, anxiety, substance use, military sexual trauma and grief care — including same-day help routes and care options that can apply even if you're not enrolled. `Free`
- **[Make the Connection](https://www.maketheconnection.net/)** — Hundreds of veterans on video, talking through problems and what treatment actually helped — a low-barrier first step, best used as a bridge to the care above, not a substitute for it. `Free`
- **[Vet Centers](https://www.vetcenter.va.gov/)** — Free, confidential, community-based counseling for combat and military-sexual-trauma readjustment, plus family and bereavement counseling — with records kept separate from VA medical records, which is the point. 24/7 Vet Center Call Center: 1-877-927-8387. `Free`
- **[Cohen Veterans Network](https://www.cohenveteransnetwork.org)** — A nonprofit clinic network offering evidence-based mental-health care, in person and by telehealth, focused on post-9/11 veterans and military families — confirm cost and coverage with the clinic; it is not VA care. `Free`
- **[Wounded Warrior Project](https://www.woundedwarriorproject.org/mission)** — Free programs for post-9/11 wounded, ill and injured veterans and their families — mental and physical wellness, connection and advocacy. Registration and eligibility apply; a charity, not a claims agency. `Free`

## 🛡️ Fraud Watch

Veterans are targeted with a specific script: charge for what's free, guarantee what's discretionary, and hold the paperwork hostage. Every entry here exists because that script works — until it's named.

- **[VBA Fraud Prevention](https://benefits.va.gov/BENEFITS/fraud-prevention.asp)** — VA's canonical warning page, in VA's own words: **do not pay anyone to help you file an initial claim** — plus how claims sharks charge multiples of an award while promising 100% ratings and faster processing, and how pension poachers hide assets in trusts and annuities they sell you. `Free`
- **[VSAFE — Veterans Scam and Fraud Evasion](https://vsafe.gov/)** — The government-wide front door for veteran-targeted fraud: learn it, detect it, report it — VSAFE Fraud Hotline **833-38V-SAFE (833-388-7233)**. Education and reporting, not benefit adjudication. `Free`
- **[VA Office of Inspector General Hotline](https://www.va.gov/oig/hotline/)** — Report fraud, waste, abuse and gross mismanagement inside VA programs (web form, or 1-800-488-8244) — note the boundaries: the OIG is not a crisis line and not an individual-claims help desk. `Free`
- **[FTC — Veterans: Don't Pay to Apply for VA Benefits](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2026/07/veterans-dont-pay-apply-va-benefits)** — The plain-language version: VA never charges to apply; only scammers guarantee benefits or a 100% rating; verify any helper in VA's accreditation search before you sign. `Free`
- **[Military Consumer](https://www.militaryconsumer.gov/)** — The FTC/DoD/CFPB-backed hub for spotting, avoiding and reporting scams aimed at servicemembers, veterans and families — complaints route onward to the FTC's reporting systems. `Free`
- **[Request military service records — National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records)** — The never-pay anchor, in NARA's words: most veterans and next of kin get DD214 copies **free**, and fee-charging "DD214 research services" are selling what the National Archives provides as a free service. (The exception is archival records — 62+ years after separation — which carry copying fees.) `Free`
